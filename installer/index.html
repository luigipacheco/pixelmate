PIXELMATE INSTALLER Source ↗

Web installer · ESP32 over USB

Put Bitsy on your board.

Plug your device in, pick it below and click install. Everything runs in this page with Web Serial: no drivers, no toolchain, nothing to download.

This browser can't talk to USB devices. Open this page in Chrome, Edge or another Chromium browser on a computer (not on a phone, and not in Safari or Firefox).
